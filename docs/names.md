# Naming candidates

Repo convention would be `SillyTavern-<Name>`; `manifest.json` `display_name` can carry a
subtitle for discoverability in ST's extension list (e.g. `"Cairn — Memory"`), so the name
itself does not need the word "memory" in it.

None of these have been checked for GitHub or ST-ecosystem collisions.

---

## Record-keeping — someone whose job is to remember

| Name | Why |
|---|---|
| **Remembrancer** | A real historical office: the official whose job is keeping what must not be forgotten. Says exactly what the extension does, and sounds like it belongs in a fantasy roleplay. |
| **Amanuensis** | One who writes on behalf of another. Captures the separation-of-responsibility rule — a second, different mind doing the writing. |
| **Commonplace** | A commonplace book is literally a curated personal store of what is worth keeping. Quiet, bookish, underused. |
| **Marginalia** | Notes in the margins of the text. Nicely describes something that sits *alongside* the roleplay rather than inside it. |
| **Chronicler** | Plain, legible, obviously about keeping a record over time. |
| **Cartulary** | A register of charters — a compiled record of what was granted and is still in force. Obscure, but the "still in force" part is precisely the canon tier. |
| **Scrivener** | Professional copyist. Slightly Bartleby-shadowed, which may be a feature or a warning. |

## Memory and cognition

| Name | Why |
|---|---|
| **Engram** | The physical trace a memory leaves. Precise, short, slightly clinical — reads as an engineering tool, not a toy. |
| **Palimpsest** | A manuscript scraped and overwritten where the earlier text is still faintly readable. The single best metaphor here for compaction-with-promotion: the old layer is gone but what mattered shows through. |
| **Anamnesis** | Recollection; the Platonic sense of remembering what was already known. Elegant, a little grand. |
| **Mnemo** | Short, obvious root, easy to type, easy to say. Safe default. |
| **Recollect** | Plain English, does what it says, reads as a verb the extension performs. |

## Strata and compaction — what settles out over time

| Name | Why |
|---|---|
| **Strata** | The tiers are literally layered, and the word carries "older is deeper and more compressed" for free. |
| **Sediment** | What settles out and hardens. The promotion mechanic in one word. |
| **Midden** | An archaeological refuse heap you reconstruct a whole civilisation from. Cheeky, memorable, and honest about what a long chat log actually is. |
| **Distillate** | The concentrated thing left after the volume is boiled off. |
| **Accretion** | Growth by slow layering. Accurate, though it leans toward "grows forever", which is the problem being solved. |

## Continuity — the film/TV sense

| Name | Why |
|---|---|
| **Continuity** | The continuity supervisor on a film set tracks what everyone is wearing, where the props are, and what time it is supposed to be — the exact job of the state tier. The best *descriptive* option on this list. |
| **Throughline** | The narrative spine that survives compression. Very roleplay-appropriate. |
| **Supervisor** | Short form of "script supervisor", same referent as Continuity. Vaguer alone. |

## Navigation and stability

| Name | Why |
|---|---|
| **Cairn** | Stacked stones left to mark a trail so you can find your way back. Sparse markers standing in for a whole route — that is the episode tier exactly. Short, clean, no baggage. |
| **Lodestone** | What you navigate by. Implies orientation rather than storage. |
| **Keel** | What keeps a boat upright and holding a course. Fits the stability/cache-prefix theme better than the memory theme. |
| **Ballast** | What keeps you stable under load. Same theme as Keel, slightly more about not capsizing than about direction. |
| **Waypoint** | Discrete recorded positions along a route. Clear but a bit generic. |

---

## Shortlist

- **Cairn** — best balance of short, evocative, unclaimed-sounding, and structurally accurate.
- **Palimpsest** — the most accurate metaphor for the core mechanic, if the length is acceptable.
- **Remembrancer** — the most characterful, and genuinely says what it does.
- **Continuity** — the most immediately legible to anyone else who reads the repo name.
- **Engram** — the most "this is a serious tool" of the short ones.
